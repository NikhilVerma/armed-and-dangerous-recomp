# 2026-10-25 (agent-cam): lev_2_m2's walk video, the camera "inside" the big rusty pipe

Symptom: the lev_2_m2 walk video (tools/compare_videos.json, W held), clip frames ~280-299 (play frames ~570-608):
the left half of the screen filled with the orange pipe's flat inside and a straight cut edge.

## What it was

Not the follow camera. Debug prints of Camera_Update's five rays (0x0040e56c, camera.rs) and of the fade
(camfade.rs) on a headless capture of the same walk (work/cam/lev_2_m2/dbg2, play frames 604..632):

- The pipe is snow_pipe 192 (p_pipes, behaviour fx_explosion). FxExplosion's load (0x0041c180) links it into World
  +0xea0, the camera rays' include list, so in the original too it fades and does not shorten the arm (only
  ownerless bodies, the landscape, stop it: RayCollector_AddBroadphaseHit 0x00413a90 with query +0x3c = 1). The
  remaster does the same: the rays hit it from t = 10.13 s (play frame ~604), its alpha falls 3 / s to the falloff
  0.3 by 10.37 s.
- The eye never enters it while it is opaque: eye-to-mesh distance (render and collision meshes, instance transform
  from scene.json, the engine's GlobalTransform checked against a logged ray hit at local radius 10.0) 7.5 at play
  frame 608, 1.2 at the closest (10.40 s, faded). A ray map of the logged camera (position, forward, up) shows only
  the pipe's outer wall in view at 608.
- The near plane is the original's (1.0: the hook's camera dumps, `"near": 1`).
- snow_pipe_1 is a thick-walled open tube (outer radius 10, bore 8, flanged rims). A faded object blends every
  front face without depth writes (the engine's plain fade; the original's too, guess: 0x004897d0 sets state bit
  0x20 for alpha < 1, Renderer_ApplyMaterial 0x00505320 sets ZWRITEENABLE = !(flags bit 0x20)), in index order, so
  at alpha 0.9 the far half of the bore and the end rims were drawn over the outer wall: the "flat interior and cut
  edge" of frame 299 is the pipe's own bore seen through its outer wall.

Why the original never showed it: its Roman stops behind the kegs (155..157) and the grunt standing on the right
one, before the pipe; the remaster's Roman passes between the kegs and the rock wall and walks up beside the pipe
(open: AI / keg placement, not the camera; see below).

## Fix (remaster, behind a setting)

`solid_fade` (graphics/geometry, Original off, Remaster on): while an object fades, each of its solid
(non-alpha-textured, non-additive) meshes gets a depth copy (camfade.rs `DepthCopy`: a child with the same mesh,
skin and a GameMaterial copy with `depth_only`: colour writes off, depth writes on, sorted 0.01 behind its colour
copy in the transparent pass), so only the nearest surface blends. The depth copy is despawned when the object is
back at 1. GameMaterial gains a pipeline key (`GameMaterialKey`) for it.

Before / after: work/cam/lev_2_m2/before_after.jpg (play frames 608, 612, 616; before = work/cam/lev_2_m2/dbg2,
after = work/cam/lev_2_m2/after; capture: compare_videos.capture_remaster with start 604 step 4).

Other levels: a scan of every walk clip for remaster frames much flatter than the original's (work/videos/frames,
local-gradient measure) found only lev_bonus_m1 288-291 (fog / bright sky) and lev_5_m1 222-231 (sky), neither a
camera-in-geometry frame.

Tests: cargo check clean; playtests camera_follow 15/15, launch_newgame 42/42, gfx_effects_remaster 16/16,
player_slope_l1m3 8/8 (--exe target-wt-cam).
