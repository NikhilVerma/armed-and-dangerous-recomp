# 2026-10-25 (move): Roman's steep slopes and wall contacts

Branch: worktree-agent-ac024f50e50543144 (merged fix/visual-bugs-1024 first). Build dir engine/target-wt-move.

## Symptom

The walk videos (tools/compare_videos.json: briefing dismissed, W held from each level's start) showed the
remaster's Roman leaving the original's path: on lev_1_m3 (from ~7 s) and lev_2_m3 he went into
`trotter_jump_idle_b` and slid back down slopes the original climbs (lev_1_m3 x 3135 -> 3086); on lev_4_m3 he stuck
against a landscape block face (x -1594.8 for good); on lev_2_m2 he stopped at y -185.5 from ~9 s.

## Cause

Roman's walker (Jolt CharacterVirtual) had max_slope_angle acos(0.7). Jolt does not make ground steeper than that
slippery: it adds a vertical constraint plane in front of it (CharacterVirtual.cpp, "Make it a vertical wall"), so
the character cannot move up it at all and slides sideways along it; the game read Jolt's OnSteepGround as "in the
air" (jump idle anim, gravity, no drive). The original has no slope limit: the player is a Havok sphere body
(PlayerObject_BuildCollision 0x00475220), the contact callback 0x00477c90 only zeroes the friction of contacts with
normal z <= 0.7, 0x00478200 counts him grounded on any up-facing contact while his vertical speed is steady
(((vz - prev) / dt)^2 < 200, vz > -5), and the drive's wanted velocity lies along the ground plane (0x004798c5:
Quat_FromTwoVectors 0x004f1830 from up to the ground normal +0x574), weakened uphill by 0x00471810's s^5. Traces
(new oracle fields roman.on_ground / floor_nz): lev_1_m3's hill has contact normals z 0.53..0.65 (58..49 degrees).

## Fix (46cda120)

- physics.rs: `Physics::walker_floor` (the last update's most upward up-facing contact normal, contacts within the
  world tolerance 0.2: Jolt keeps a resting walker up to ~0.5 units up as a predictive contact); `add_walker`'s
  max_slope 0 means no limit.
- player.rs: Roman's walker has no slope limit; `grounded` is 0x00478200's rule; on steep ground (nz <= 0.7) no
  friction, gravity acts, the body keeps the velocity the contact left it, the wanted velocity is turned along the
  slope, and the drive is released when the 0.5 s grace runs out while touching (0x004799d5). Walkable ground keeps
  the verified walker behaviour (walk speed, landing, camera tests unchanged).
- charanim.rs: jump idle only in the jump state (game_05.cpp:1885-1899).
- Test: engine/playtests/player_slope_l1m3.json (in regress SUITE): passes on the fix, fails 3 of 8 on the old build.

## Learned

- Jolt's slope limit is a wall, not a slide (docs/remaster-engine.md trap).
- lev_4_m3's reeds (g_lev_4_m3_reeds01, p_x_4_4_branch collide_poly concave) are crossed double-sided grass cards
  with poly collision; one card's edge stands 1.5 units from the block face Roman runs along.
