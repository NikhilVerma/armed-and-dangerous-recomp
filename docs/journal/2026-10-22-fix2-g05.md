# 2026-10-22 fix2 g05: companions and Roman's AI noise (audit wave 2)

Branch agent-fix2-g05 (from d28f04b). Work package work/audit/wave2/g05.json: 59 findings, owned file
engine/src/ai.rs; small stated hooks in engine/src/vehicles/rockets.rs (the end noise of Roman's rockets) and
engine/src/player.rs (no end noise when a bullet's lifetime runs out), tools/regress.py (suite entries).

Outcome: 32 fixed, 3 false claims, 2 unreachable in data, 6 already correct, 16 deferred. One original bug logged
(docs/original-bugs.md: the companions' retaliation window, 0x00486df0).

What changed in play:
- Roman's Fire no longer makes Q and Jonesy shoot. In formation they reload in place (facing within 45 degrees of
  Roman's heading) and turn to his heading (0x00485580 / 0x00486525; the 0x28..0x2b anims are the reload set,
  which has no fire notes).
- They follow on the original's radius: Q when Roman is more than 27.5 away, Jonesy 52.5, or when the partner asks.
  Turning in place no longer makes them move. A formation point with no floor or no route keeps them where they are.
- "Return To Me" / "Defend Me" toggled while idle keeps them in place; from a fight or an area / attack order they
  run back at once. The attack order ends, silently, as soon as its target is gone and the fight is over (no extra
  3..7 s at the attack point). A Defend Area order shows the fx_team_mates marker.
- An idle companion joins the partner's fight when the target is within reach; targets beyond 2 x scan_distance are
  dropped; the anchor test is applied after choosing the nearest grunt.
- Hits on Roman make a companion retaliate only within 10 s of his last offered target (the original's test), never
  under the attack order. Attackers that hit a companion are offered with the original's gates.
- Q offers the tea only when idle in formation (not mid-run or mid-fight); also when Roman is already dead.
- Grunts hear Roman's Gurner / vindaloo / forge-cannon shells land (radius 200, 2 s), and the vindaloo's flight.
  A bullet that expires in the air makes no end noise.
