## Learned
- Judge "same or better" by eye before hunting bugs: the first sets passed a bug hunt yet were blurrier (wrong UV set
  for detail maps), which only a subjective side-by-side showed.
- A capture full of near-camera characters needs every frame of the last second checked, not just f299: the fade
  that hid Q also hid that his route was wrong.
- Check a fix with targeted renders of the affected shots before a full recapture; full recaptures went stale twice.
