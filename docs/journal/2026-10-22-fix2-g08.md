## Learned

- 0x0047bc80(10) / ToggleZoom: the Controls page's action table 0x00588dd8 (read from the exe) is
  {0..9, -, -, -, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 12, 11, 18, 19, 20}: Prev Weapon (0xf) and Prev Special (0x11) are bindable, ToggleZoom
  (0xa) is not. Its default is unbound, so the toggle form is unreachable on PC.
- The zoom is the camera "fade" 0x0040fbb0: reaching 1 hides the player (vt+0xf4(0)) and his weapons (0x004a0e00(0)).
- 0x004a10e0 recreates the weapon object on every switch; its last-fire time +0x544 starts at 0, so a gun switched back in is
  ready at once.
- p_player_machinegun_bullet and the mounted guns' bullets have no allow_cartoon_death key: code default true (0x004534f0 al = 1),
  so their records carry 0x80 (window grunts killed by them are blown out). Only the rifle and sniper bullets set 0.
- p_captain_sniperbullet has head_shot_multiplier 5: a head hit on Roman does 300.
