## Found (evidence)

- **Characters collide with each other in the original**: lev_2_m2 grunt 16 (radius 4, state 0x96) walks round grunt 15
  at 7.7..7.97 apart (the trace), pushing him 0.03. 0x00411850 is not a pair filter for the simulation (the game asks it
  in the swept-body listener 0x004df8a0; as a pair filter it would refuse a character against the landscape).
- **The owner veto** of CollisionFilter_IsEnabled 0x00411710 for AI characters is GenericAIObject_Slot40 0x00432590:
  never the +0x50c object, never the same +0x510 group. 0x0042de60 (joining a squad) sets +0x510 = the squad's
  -squad_tag; nothing clears it on a charge (0x0042cc10). Slot44 0x004320b0 (the per-step contact note) sets +0x510 = 0
  and +0x50c = the mover touched by a walker with a route (not 0x96 / 0x97 / 0x10f). The original's squad members
  overlap (lev_bonus_m3: ~3 units apart at frame 58).
- **Friction**: 0 while in a squad (0x0042de30 / 0x0042de60 / ai_06.cpp:2650), on marker splines with +0x488 bit 16
  (0x0042d1b0), on the squad anchor; the material's (0x0042cb80) otherwise and on leaving the squad (0x0042cc10);
  combined sqrt(fA * fB) (0x00413240).
- **Landing**: a flight lands on the contact note (any contact, Slot44 -> 0x0042e1c0), not on ground under the feet.
- **The drive** (0x00454d80 / AIRoute_Steer 0x00470910): a force reaching the wanted velocity in one 0.02 s action
  (gain mass * 50, duration mass / (mass * 50)), z kept when falling: equivalent to the remaster's velocity set.
- **force_ground's tolerance** is 0.3: the original lifts grunt 44 / captain 66 to 0.31 / 0.32 off the overhang
  (z 315.99 / 297.89); 0.1 left them 0.11 / 0.12 off (z 1.4 / 0.9 lower).
- **B3b** (open-questions.md): grunt 44's body touches the landscape (owner 0) from play frame 3, every frame; from
  frame 5 its velocity is exactly 0 while awake; no game call sets it. The added contact (new hook option
  `trace_contacts`) has normal (0.2457, -0.9589, 0.1417) and w -0.68 against an 82-degree face 4.29 from his centre,
  from behind. A turned-round landscape copy in Jolt let him slide (~4.5 units/s) and cost ~12% frame time: dropped.
