## Done

  sphere of sphere_radius centred that high above him (0x00405880 -> 0x004574e0); the contact slot (0x00406210)
  captures him (fewer than three carried, not attached) and sends on_enter once; the update (0x00405d10) ties him as a
  ragdoll and runs the rope chain (+0x480 places, +0x478 rope reeling 5 / s to 3, a Havok hard key-frame of the pelvis
  toward the leader's "center"); the mission's peasant_saved_trigger handler (0x0044d600) banks the chain's last at
  the mat that sent it and runs the mat's death slot (a one-shot mat banks one and dies); the kill slot (0x004062a0)
  counts the casualties and loses the level (state 8). Peasants are on none of the damage lists (bullets and blasts
  pass). The squad goliaths' cage peasants are held (Object_Attach flags 0x14) and fall free when the goliath dies.
