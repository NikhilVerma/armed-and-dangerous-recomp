# 2026-10-17 agent-corpses: the dead's bodies (corpse timer, fade, removal, ragdoll limit, remaster keep)

## The problem, measured

A lev_2_m4 run of the old build (ENTDBG breakdown, `corpses_l2m4_long` script: turret aimed at the army, 23 kills):
3.7k entities at the start, 50.7k at 40 s, 84.4k at 60 s, 115k at 100 s, 126k at 150-190 s; frames 17 -> 70-120 ms
(headless 640x372). The bulk is not corpses: 1,745 live army members (MemberRun, ~70 entities each: a skinned
low-detail grunt with its rig). lev_2_m4's five army timers' anims (army_timer_l5_m5_1..5, 133 s) hold 357
create_platoon notes, made in the first ~2 minutes, and while the live army grunts sit at the grunt limit nothing is
released, so the members pile up. The original draws them as particles. Open (not this task): a cheap member.

The corpse leak itself: no dead character was ever removed. Each death kept its whole entity tree (~70) and, for a
ragdoll, its 11 Jolt bodies for the rest of the level; topsy-thrown grunts stayed hidden; critters were hidden, not
removed; Physics::remove left the body's material / no_bullets entries.
