What changed in play:
- A firing grunt (and one standing up in his slot) aims with the four-pose set: inside 45 degrees of his heading his
  body stays and the upper body follows the target (the weights step at dt / (2 x 45 x 0.0037)); beyond it he turns at
  0x00454e60's rate until he faces it (within about 11.5 degrees), then aims again.
- The shared turn (ai/body.rs `turn_toward`, 0x00454e60): rate x sin(off) rad/s. Q / Jonesy at the fight point turn
  that way and start the kill once they face it (dot >= 0.98); the twiglets turn at 16 (was an exponential at 8); the
  goliath turns to Roman at 4 through its pick-up and throw instead of snapping.
- The gunner's led aim (counters ran out together) is measured and led from the gun's muzzle, starting from Roman's
  ref_hit_00.
- A companion stunned during a close kill hangs at once; a held grunt knocked out of the hold (topsy) lets his
  companion go into his reload. The close kill's try follows the original's order (the coin before the grunt's
  answer, the route and the answer inside the try: a refusal leaves the engage to its TNT roll); ledge_death and
  scared-run grunts refuse.
- A walking grunt that bumps any dynamic body (Roman's vehicle, a ragdoll, a loose prop) passes through it from then
  on, not only other grunts; a marker walker re-plans instead.
- Tree cover: the covering ray starts at the slot point and counts only the tree (the original's include-object
  query); the grunt at a tree slot faces away from the target (the slot's direction as 0x00497210 returns it).
- The wall assault: a climber who leaves his way (spline dropped outside a script) frees it; a freed place is
  searched for in the same frame; new ladder carriers never take a path with a ladder already planted.
