# 2026-10-22: accuracy audit wave 2, package g02 (agent-fix2-g02)

Checked the 36 findings of work/audit/wave2/g02.json (engine/src/ai.rs: target offers and hand-overs, triggers, the
sticky scare, the turn to the target) against the current code (base d28f04b). Reachability was checked over the 24
level exports (instance_props.json / properties.json / mission.json under work/remaster).

Files outside ai.rs (small hooks): level.rs (`SpawnGrunt::priority_tags`, set on the troop), alarm.rs (the generator's
priority tags into the spawn), mission.rs (the barracks read priority_target_tag_min / max and pass them).

## Player-visible changes

- Alarm and barracks troops get their generator's priority targets (0x0042cc60): lev_4_m2's alarm troops go for
  Jonesy (tag 600), lev_5_m2's barracks captains for Roman (tag 1), lev_bonus_m2 / m3's troops for their tags. The
  priority walk now also finds placed characters (Roman, Jonesy, Q), not only destructible objects.
- Grunts with receive_trigger_kill die when the trigger is sent (0x0042f870): lev_2_m2 (900, 7 grunts), lev_4_m2 (802,
  15), lev_4_m3 (2345, 18).
- lev_4_m1's monk 536 (trigger_charge, trigger_no_damage) cannot be hurt until his trigger 4001 (0x004371e0).
- Sticky bomb scare (0x0042fb60): the grunt the bomb is on runs 250 from Roman (was 100); the ones near run 100 from
  the bomb's object; busy grunts (on a gun, waiting for a trigger, carried, held, ...) and runners already fleeing are
  not scared again; guards near the bomb (not on them) stay silent.
- A grunt busy at a hit (0x0042f8d0: waiting for his trigger, an IC move, held, on a gun, on the way to a spline's head,
  marching in a squad) plays no hit reaction and does not retaliate; a waiting grunt wakes on a hit only with
  trigger_los, with the shooter as his target.
- Group alerts (0x0042cc70 / 0x0042c700): every group mate gets the hand-over, also one holding a target; its end is a
  real offer (0x0042c820: the 10 s hold, the nearer test, the stamp); a target taken by retaliation alerts the group
  too; traitors are passed over.
- Guards: the infinite-accuracy ranges are no longer used whenever the target is beyond range; a guard takes them, with
  99999 as both ranges, for good only when an offer finds his target beyond his range (0x0042c790). So guards are less
  accurate at long range until shot from afar, then they engage at any range. Guards also drop a target beyond 2 x
  scan_distance (0x0042ec20).
- The fire-state turn toward the target uses 0x00454e60's rate (16 x sin of the angle, rad/s); the turn anims do not
  start while a shot's fire anim or the reload set plays.
- Start-in-turret levels: the target search re-arms every 2..4 s (0x0044e600 branch of AI_FindTarget).
