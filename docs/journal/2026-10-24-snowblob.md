# 2026-10-24: the snow blobs near the camera (player feedback G6)

The human, on docs/images/preview.jpg: "why did we add back those screen space snow blobs in the remaster".

## Done

- **What draws them:** not the GPU snow (graphics/snow.rs) but the original's camera-locked flake emitter: an
  invisible `fxo_glow_red` that fx_camera_l1_m1_locked attaches to the camera, spraying soft sprites along the view
  at the lens for the whole level (docs/subsystems/weather.md "snow bubbles"; fxvm particle pool 1).
- **Why they came back:** the player reported these "bubbles" in the hook era and the old remaster muted the emitter
  together with its GPU snow (67d7adf, archive/hook/enhance/weather.cpp `FxUpdateCheck`). The Bevy port made them
  visible again on purpose: 4374220 (FX1, 10-18: alpha no longer raised to 2.2, emitter restarted after loads; at
  the time the port had no GPU snow and the domes were too faint) and the GPU snow port (FX4) kept the emitter
  ("FX1 wants its flakes"). G1 (626203b) documented them as a known difference instead of removing them.
- **Fix:** `SnowTuning::mutes` (default `["fxo_glow_red"]`): while the GPU snowfall runs, the fx objects of those
  templates locked to the snow camera's full transform stop emitting (`FxObject::muted`,
  `FxVm::mute_camera_emitters`); particles already out finish. Roman's footstep puffs (same template, attached to
  characters) are untouched. The Original preset keeps the original's puffs. Playtest state `snow.emitters_muted`.
- Tests: gpu_snow, gpu_snow_original, fx_snow (moved to the Original preset, where the emitter is the spec),
  gfx_effects_remaster PASS (target-wt-snowblob). Clippy: no errors, no warnings in the touched files.
- Shots (1920x1080, gallery cameras l1_start / l1_village, `snow_tuning={"mutes":[]}` as "before"):
  work/snowfix/*_before_after.jpg.
