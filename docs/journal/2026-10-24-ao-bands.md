# 2026-10-24: the dark bands in the walk videos (agent, branch worktree-agent-a5eeac479b887b0b4)

## Done

The human saw large black hard-edged polygons and horizontal dark bands in the Remaster walk videos (lev_2_m3,
lev_3_m3, lev_4_m1, lev_5_m2) and on the village houses of the hero image (lev_1_m1: "dark bottom is grey").

- Isolated by toggling at the walk videos' play frames (1920x1080, the gallery's Remaster settings): fog_modern off
  keeps the bands, ambient_occlusion off removes them, ao_radius 4 keeps them (not a radius effect), ao_contact 0
  removes them (the contact term darkens the sun's light too, so the bad AO turned sunlit ground dark).
- The raw SSAO term drawn in the game material: a flat wall about 0.85 above a hard line, about 0.13 below it.
  Depth prepass equal to the fragment's depth, but the prepass normal disagreed with the vertex normal (|delta| ~1.4).
  The prepass normals drawn: a smooth sky-dome gradient over every solid object, the landscape's "up" where it was
  drawn after.
- Cause: graphics/farclip.rs `specialize` tested for Bevy's prepass with `value_as_string() == "PREPASS_PIPELINE"`;
  the value of a flag definition is "true", so the test never matched and the sky's prepass pipeline lost its depth
  test as well as the colour pass. The sky then wrote its normals (and motion vectors) over the solid scenery drawn
  before it in the prepass. Fixed by matching the definition's name; unit test `prepass_pipeline_by_name`.
- Roman: not this cause. His brightness is the same within 1-5 % before and after (crops of the f10 frames); his
  darker look than the original is something else (character shading), not investigated.
