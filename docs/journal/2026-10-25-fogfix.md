## Done

- **C1, the grey rain dome (lev_5_m1, lev_5_m3; player feedback G5).** `--debug-hide` bisection: the camera's
  wind-spray dome fxo_wind_spray (fxlayer.wesl) makes the veil and the blotchy teal patterns; the rain domes
  fxo_rain_1..3 (fxadd.wesl) brighten the rest. Cause: the original adds these (SRCALPHA / ONE, 0x004897d0) to a
  gamma-space back buffer; fxlayer added its gamma colour as linear light (fx_wind_spray up to 41/255: over a dark
  pixel that is a gamma +0.4 lift), fxadd matched over the snow levels' 0.6. Fix: fx objects following the camera's
  camera_fx (the camera or a triggered camera_fx's holder, mission.rs) read Bevy's transmission texture (a copy of
  the opaque pass) and add exactly `(g + c)^2.2 - g^2.2` over their own pixel (fxvm/over_scene.wesl). The camera
  carries `ScreenSpaceTransmission { steps: 1, quality: Low }` (graphics/mod.rs): one copy a frame, only while such
  a dome is in view. lev_5_m3 f060 / f150 and lev_5_m1 f150: bridge, tower and Roman clear, light rain.

## Learned

- A custom Bevy material can read the opaque scene by returning true from `reads_view_transmission_texture`
  (Transmissive3d phase, needs `ScreenSpaceTransmission` on the camera, `@if(VIEW_TRANSMISSION_TEXTURE)` in WESL).
  It is the clean way to emulate the original's gamma-space blending per pixel.
- The fxadd / particle "matched over 0.6" approximation is fine over snow but far too bright over dark scenes;
  other additive fx (flames, glows) could use over_scene.wesl too (section A's call: it moves them into the
  transmissive phase, drawn before the transparent one).
