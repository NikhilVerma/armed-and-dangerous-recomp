## Learned

- Roman carries three guns and the special list holds five; the pub swaps (0x004a0f70) when the three are full.
- The original's "Guy Fawkes" is the Traitor Bomb (texts: weap_guyfawkes): 0x00430140 swaps the AI's two lists; the
  feature row said "set alight" (corrected).
- The shark gun fires only with a target (0x004a2910); "armed jaws" is the shark projectile's own class / loop sound.
- The vindaloo's rocket explodes when it starts to move away from its target (a proximity fuse, 0x004805f0) and gives
  the target the direct damage then.
- The black hole carries AIs off with damage record flag 0x800 (the holder: the hole), the AI states 0x19c / 0x19d run
  from it, then pull them in; World +0xf00 (the sucking holes) also steers every projectile and pulls Roman.
- Each weapon id's ammo record is filled at the level's start (player_set_ammo_* default -2), held or not: a gun bought
  at the pub comes full.
- Scripted runs on levels with companions: Q and Jonesy kill spawned grunts within seconds; weapon tests that count
  kills fire within 0.3 s of the spawn or use a level without them (lev_4_m3).
