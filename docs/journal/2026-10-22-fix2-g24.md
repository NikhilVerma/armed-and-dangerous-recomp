## Learned

- The window's 32 glass hit nodes are tracks of its anim (base_turret_l5_m5_window), not skeleton bones; the turret's
  own ref_hit_00..06 are its AI aim points.
- +0xa18 bit 0 (0x00426630) is "in an IC move": the run-over targets the wall scalers.
- The scratchpad folder is shared by every session of this project: build logs need unique names (another agent's
  build overwrote build.log mid-run).
