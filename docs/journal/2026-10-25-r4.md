# 2026-10-25: visual bugs round 4 (block face, hit markers, ground mask, LOD sill, snow facets)

Branch worktree-agent-a23ed398d4792ae2e (merged fix/visual-bugs-1024 first). This round found causes but changed no
engine code. Two items are faithful to the original, one is an original art defect, and the other two belong to
the uv-set / tangent work running in parallel (agent-a2cd06b8f6b00a709, uncommitted at the time; told by message).
Captures are headless, with the walk-video keys and preset, frames numbered as in work/videos/frames, in
work/r4/<level>/<tag>/. Evidence sheets are in work/r4/sheets/.

## Findings

1. **lev_4_m3 black block face (frames ~120-299): not a rendering bug.** The face is landscape submesh b_stone_dark,
   the plane x = -1597 with normal +x (found by ray-searching the landscape around Roman's traced position
   -1594.8, 1753.6). lev_4_m3 has only two lights: the sun (255, 253, 188), toward (-0.22, 0.73, 0.65), and the fill
   (77, 104, 111) x 0.7, toward (0.20, -0.98, 0.07). It has **no AmbientOnly light**. LightObject_Load 0x0044add0
   fills D3DLIGHT9.Ambient only for AmbientOnly lights, and Renderer_UpdateLightConstants 0x004ff2e0 sends c3 =
   material ambient x the sum of the lights' ambient + emissive = 0. So the face gets N.L(sun) = 0 and 0.2 of the fill:
   about 5/255 in the remaster, in both presets. Ruled out: AO off, shadow_strength 0, fog off (AND_DEBUG_NOFOG),
   original textures, Original preset (all 5.4). At frame 120 the same block's sunlit (0.71, 0.70) face, in shadow,
   measures 23 in the remaster and 25.6 in the original. The real difference is the path. The original's Roman leaves
   the bank into the water. The remaster's Roman is caught against this face: 13 units from play frame 250 to 400
   while running. That puts the camera on a face the original's camera never shows. Sheet: item1_lev_4_m3_block_face.jpg.
2. **lev_bonus_m1 / l1_courtyard red band and red streak: the original's hit markers.** They vanish with --no-hud.
   They are ui_hit_top and ui_hit_right (mesh b_hit, 0x004670f0, additive at 0.5, docs/subsystems/hud.md). They show
   for damage_flash_duration on a hit from that side. The original's walk video shows the same pair at lev_bonus_m1
   frame 132 (found by scanning every original frame for red at the top). At frame 299 the original's Roman is
   already dead. Sheet: item2_lev_bonus_m1_hit_markers.jpg.
3. **lev_bonus_m1 dark parallelograms and diagonal line: the terrain mask on the wrong uv set.** Hiding objects
   changes nothing. The sun-ray map (landscape plus placed objects) shows no caster over the courtyard. The ground is
   lev_3_m3's material (lev_3_m3_ground_2 / _1, mask lev_3_m3_ground_mask, kind 13) with uv_sets 0x90, and its mask is
   a whole-level map on uv set 2. uvs2 runs 0..1; uvs1 runs 1.7..8.7 on lev_bonus_m1 and -2..21 on lev_3_m3.
   gamematerial.wesl samples the mask with uv_b (set 1), so lev_3_m3's building footprints (black) and road lines
   tile about 7x over the arena. lev_3_m3's own ground has the same fault. The parallel uv-set work (its diff:
   MaterialDef::uv_sets, uv_of(), ATTRIBUTE_UV_2) is the place to fix it, but its uv_of maps every set >= 1 to set 1,
   so the game material still needs set 2. Not edited here to avoid two implementations. Sheet:
   item3_lev_bonus_m1_mask_uvset.jpg.
4. **lev_4_m3 white rectangles on the middle house: original art exposed by lod_max.** This is ww_house_2f_b_0's
   inward-facing sill over an opening, and the fog / sky (172, 189, 190) shows through. Model draws cull CW: cull index
   1, table 0x005ca798 = {1, 2, 3} read from the exe. With lod_max=false the remaster draws LOD _1 like the original
   and the slit is gone. Logged in docs/original-bugs.md. Sheets: item4_lev_4_m3_lod0_sill.jpg and
   item4_ww_house_2f_b_{0,1}_front.png.
5. **lev_1_m3 snow facets: in the original too, sharper in the remaster.** The slope is landscape submeshes 3 / 4
   (snow_nonpath_ground1 / snow_path_ground1, the same material). Submesh 3 has per-triangle rotated uv0: about 40 %
   of its triangles near (3110, 1100) have tangent directions spread over all headings, against one direction in
   submesh 4. Each of those triangles shows the base texture and n_b_t_snow's relief turned, so the light falls on
   it differently. The original shows the same facet (equalized frame 120, item5_lev_1_m3_facets.jpg). The remaster's
   seams are harder because its tangents come from MikkTSpace, averaged over vertices shared by turned and unturned
   triangles. The original stores its own tangent / binormal (stream 1, skin slots 14 / 15, docs/formats/big-skin.md),
   which the exporter does not decode yet. That is the faithful fix, and it is in the same parallel work (tangents
   along uv sets).

## Learned

- `--debug-hide NAME` and `NEAR_DUMP=<seconds>` (main.rs) find what is drawn at a pixel. Hiding the HUD
  (`--no-hud`) is the first test for anything flat and screen-aligned.
- A material's `uv_sets` byte (+0x32) matters for terrain too: lev_3_m3's town ground puts its mask on set 2.
