## Why the bot sat at the guard camp (level1u rerun, 767 s, timed out)

From engine/playtests/level1u.log.jsonl of the failed run (the main checkout's copy):
- It barely hit anything: 1795 shots, 26 kills. The bot set Roman's look straight at the target from the eye
  (pitch - 0.08, undone by player.rs aim) and its shots skipped the crosshair: they left the hand parallel to the eye
  line, a few units off it, and missed at range. The previous win had 1339 shots, 69 hits.
- So fights dragged on, health ran down (lowest 25), and at 75 health ("low") every sight of a grunt meant
  back_off or a hiding cover that saw nobody, then "nothing to shoot for 2 s", leave, seen again, cover again: 290 s
  of to_cover / cover / back_off / route at the camp with health unchanged (nobody was even hitting it).
- Between those, nav routes cut corners past the pine tree at (62, -482) and stuck repeatedly.

## Fixes

- Aim through the crosshair (`CameraRig::look_at`), fire only while the crosshair's point is on the target, skip
  targets it cannot reach. Pub leg from a cold start: pub at 63 s (was 102), 40 shots, 15 kills, health 390.
- Siren: shot by the bot (raised sirens are targets within 400 in sight); `destroy 33` removed from level1u.
- Keg chains: `blast_reaches` (a sticky's or keg's blast through the kegs it sets off). The first siren-leg run died
  at full health: a sticky at a group set off the three kegs by the siren (150 each) 30 units from Roman.
- Use held: stand still (a duel side-step walked Roman off the gate-1 ticker site; the bomb was never set).
- Stuck handling: escape turns round the full circle, a jump at 2 s and every 4 s (not every stuck frame: it jumped
  onto a wall top by gate 1 and dropped into a pocket at game (-78, -42) it could not leave, frozen 200 s).
- nav.rs: a cost for steps without room for Roman's width (routes hugged wall 150's end beside pine 73 at
  (-157..-155, -96..-101) and wedged him); waypoints kept where a straight walk would brush something; NAVMAP debug map.
- Script: gate 1 opens to the west (the gate spans y -19..101 at x -88): waypoints (-55, 30), (-120, 25) before
  (-160, -14) (from (0, 150) the planner ended in the yard south of the gate); (-160, -96) dropped (the wall end).
