## Learned

- An IC path in state 0x64 is a velocity chase, not a placement: anything driven that way (vehicles and, through
  ai/ic.rs, characters) lags its anim by about a second at speed.
- Object reference matrices come from the template's anim slot (templates.json default_anim), not the model's name;
  most templates use the model's name for both, invisible_barracks does not.
- The oracle harness's frame is 16.6667 ms, not 1/60 s.
