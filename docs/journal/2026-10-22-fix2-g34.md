## Learned

- `StopSound(name)` stops every voice of that name. A per-object loop needs a `SoundId` and `StopVoice`; Roman's glide
  loop used to stop the captains' loops too.
- bevy_enhanced_input accumulates bindings cumulatively, but an action-level axial `DeadZone` clamps each axis to 1,
  which matches the original's per-binding max in practice.
