## Done

- **Shadow pop-in.** Captured with the new `tools/visual_capture.py` (the player's settings, a scripted walk, frame
  dumps) and `tools/contact_sheet.py`. Terrain shadows vanished as the camera turned: casters 100-200+ units behind
  or beside the camera were culled (cull_radius 100, shadow range 200, low sun). The default is now 400; at
  300/400 the same walk is stable.
- **LOD.** `[render] lod_max` (remaster on): `Object_SelectLod` 0x00452f50 at distance 0 (enhancement point
  `g_lodForceFinest`).
- **Draw distance.** `[render] draw_distance` (1.0 = the game's maximum far scale, up to 4x; enhancement point
  `g_cameraFarScaleAdjust` in 0x0040f8c0).
- **Anisotropic filtering.** Already forced to 16x on mipmapped, linearly filtered samplers (vk_pipeline.cpp).
- **AO.**
  - The motion target is now RGBA16F: every opaque pixel writes motion, AO weight and albedo.
  - The game's three pixel shaders (all ps_1_1) report their albedo and ambient share. The sun term, the v1 glow and
    specular are not occluded.
  - Multi-bounce uses the real albedo.
  - Snow-white surfaces keep 25% of the occlusion (`ao_snow`).
  - XeGTAO constants come from the settings. The radius was 2.5, which left the term nearly white at the game's
    scale; it is now 24.
  - `ao_debug` views.
- **Soft particles** (agent, merged).
  - Particles are detected by wrapping the scene pass's `Fx_DrawParticles` 0x00423820 and `Streamers_Draw`
    0x00494f80. Decals and other blended layers must not fade.
  - Depth is read-only while sampled.
  - The fade depends on the blend mode; range 2.
  - The foot snow puff's cut line is gone.
- **Display.** Borderless and fullscreen no longer offer modes wider than the monitor: 4096x2160 had been squeezed
  into 3840x2160.
- **Temporal AA, FSR 3.1** in the 64-bit helper (docs/subsystems/remaster-aa.md). The plumbing is verified; the image
  quality is not, because Smart App Control blocks fresh unsigned builds.

## Shadow flicker while moving (the player's report)

- **Measurement.** tools/shadow_flicker.py measures the shadow term: shadowed frames divided by unshadowed frames of
  the same deterministic run. With the original caster culling (cull_radius 0, the culling the player last played
  with on 2026-10-04), walking and turning popped up to 43.8% of the screen in a single frame. With the current
  default (400) the worst frame is 7.5%, and consecutive frames look stable. Walking alone toggles 0.26% of pixels
  per frame.
- **Ray tracing off by default.** The player wants stable shadows first, so `shadow_rt` is now opt-in.
- **Helper readiness.** `RtClient::Ready` now waits briefly for the helper instead of falling back to shadow maps.
  A fallback counter is logged per 300 frames.
- Deployed as f904d2d.

## Soft shadow seam (PCSS)

- **Symptom.** With the default `shadow_softness=0.6` a bright speckled line ran along the outer edge of every soft
  shadow (work/visual/sh_soft2, lev_1_m1 cliff shadow at frame 660; absent at softness 0.05).
- **Cause.** The blocker search looked 6 texels out (a fallback search at a quarter of the cap), but the filter
  reached further. A pixel just outside the shadow whose filter disc still touched the occluder found no blocker
  and was drawn fully lit, beside neighbours blurred to partial shadow.
- **Fix** (src/rhi/vulkan/vk_shadows.cpp). The first search covers the whole penumbra cap with 32 spiral taps, and a
  second pass refines over the implied penumbra. The penumbra cap is 16 texels (about 1.6 world units at the default
  range): the blocker-distance estimate over-reads under cliffs, and 256 texels washed out shadow interiors.
- **Evidence.** work/visual/sh_fix_zoom3.png (hard, old soft, new soft): seam gone, interior as dark as the hard
  shadow. Flicker unchanged on the turn sequence (fl_maps2 0.938% vs fl_maps3 0.937% toggling pixels per frame).
  `compare.py --scenario level,play` PASS, check.ps1 GREEN.
