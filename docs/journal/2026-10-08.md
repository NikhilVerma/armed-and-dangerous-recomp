# 2026-10-08

## Direction

The user's priority is a modern, playable game through the hook route, keeping Havok. Jolt is paused (stage 1
shadow done). Requested:
- shadow pop-in fixed;
- temporal AA (DLAA / FSR / XeSS) from libraries;
- anisotropic filtering always 16x;
- no LOD;
- maximum draw distance;
- better AO (too weak, wrongly darkens snow);
- particles cut by the floor.

`wip/full-decomp` was merged into main (e7e0296).

## Done

- **Shadow pop-in.** Captured with the new `tools/visual_capture.py` (the player's settings, a scripted walk, frame
  dumps) and `tools/contact_sheet.py`. Terrain shadows vanished as the camera turned: casters 100-200+ units behind
  or beside the camera were culled (cull_radius 100, shadow range 200, low sun). The default is now 400; at
  300/400 the same walk is stable.
- **LOD.** `[render] lod_max` (remaster on): `Object_SelectLod` 0x00452f50 at distance 0 (enhancement point
  `g_lodForceFinest`).
- **Draw distance.** `[render] draw_distance` (1.0 = the game's maximum far scale, up to 4x; enhancement point
  `g_cameraFarScaleAdjust` in 0x0040f8c0).
- **Anisotropic filtering.** Already forced to 16x on mipmapped, linearly filtered samplers (vk_pipeline.cpp).
- **AO.**
  - The motion target is now RGBA16F: every opaque pixel writes motion, AO weight and albedo.
  - The game's three pixel shaders (all ps_1_1) report their albedo and ambient share. The sun term, the v1 glow and
    specular are not occluded.
  - Multi-bounce uses the real albedo.
  - Snow-white surfaces keep 25% of the occlusion (`ao_snow`).
  - XeGTAO constants come from the settings. The radius was 2.5, which left the term nearly white at the game's
    scale; it is now 24.
  - `ao_debug` views.
- **Soft particles** (agent, merged).
  - Particles are detected by wrapping the scene pass's `Fx_DrawParticles` 0x00423820 and `Streamers_Draw`
    0x00494f80. Decals and other blended layers must not fade.
  - Depth is read-only while sampled.
  - The fade depends on the blend mode; range 2.
  - The foot snow puff's cut line is gone.
- **Display.** Borderless and fullscreen no longer offer modes wider than the monitor: 4096x2160 had been squeezed
  into 3840x2160.
- **Temporal AA, FSR 3.1** in the 64-bit helper (docs/subsystems/remaster-aa.md). The plumbing is verified; the image
  quality is not, because Smart App Control blocks fresh unsigned builds.

## Learned

- Smart App Control is on (enforcement) on the player's PC. It blocks fresh unsigned binaries by cloud reputation,
  intermittently: rt_helper.exe (error 4551), vk_shader_test.exe, and once dinput8.dll ("Bad Image" 0xc0e90002).
  Code Integrity log events 3077 / 3118. This is the user's setting.
- Filmic ACES (`tonemap_aces`) washes the game's LDR-authored colours out and also hits the HUD. It is not a default.
