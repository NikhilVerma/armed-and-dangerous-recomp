# 2026-10-22: accuracy audit wave 2, package g01 (agent-fix2-g01)

The 42 findings of work/audit/wave2/g01.json (engine/src/ai.rs: the grunts' fire cycle, engage, hiding-spot search,
pursuit and group alerts), checked against the recovered C++ and the level data under work/remaster/. Base d28f04b.

What a player sees:

- Captains, Q112s and other grunts with fire_num_bursts > 1 fire short bursts inside one clip, with the burst delay
  between them, instead of whole clips of rounds x bursts shots. Example: p_captain fires 15..20 shots per clip in
  bursts of 1..5, not 15..100 shots.
- Grunts with clips left reload in place and fire again. They no longer look for new cover after every clip.
  Guards do not taunt.
- A grunt that loses sight of his target while firing chases it. The chase engages only when its 0.2..0.4 s sight
  ray finds the target in range. The grunt no longer stands still behind a tree trying to fire.
- Captains standing up in a trench can throw a grenade, at the same 15 % engage roll used in the open.
- Forge cannon gunners fire one shell per sequence, then wait forge_fire_delay (0.5..2 s). Before, they used the
  machine gun's 3..5 clips of 10 rounds.
- In the base-defence levels the attackers' cover search no longer takes guns, trenches or tree slots.
- A group alert also reaches members that already have a target. When the hand-over ends, the member switches only
  if the new target is nearer. Jonesy does not throw TNT at a grunt in an IC move or one being held for a close kill.
