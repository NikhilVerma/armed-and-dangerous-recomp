## Learned
- Bevy 0.20 shaders are WESL (`import bevy_pbr::render::forward_io::VertexOutput;`, `constants::MATERIAL_BIND_GROUP`);
  a WGSL `#import` fails to parse.
- Script setters must reach objects created in the same run: Bevy's commands are deferred, so the fx VM owns its
  objects' state instead of reading components.
- The benchmark's own 4K screenshots cost ~100 ms (PNG encoding after the GPU readback): skip 0.3 s after each.
- A GPU shared with another job halves the 4K numbers: rerun before believing a regression.
