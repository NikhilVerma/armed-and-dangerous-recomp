## What was learned

- Several labels of the deferred rows were wrong: 0x00428220 is the "invincible" / "vulnerable" event (only the planted
  ladder's anim has the note); 0x0042f470 is the punch launch, not a squad join (new feature punch-launch, the
  player's side); 0x0042db00 is the pre-success retreat; the fake grunt helpers are the squads'.
- The "16 %" rolls of 0x00427910 / 0x00427e80 are 50 % (the constant 0x190000 is 50 << 15).
- Python's text-mode writes turn LF files into CRLF on Windows and Git Bash's `sed -i` dropped a CRLF file's CRs once:
  edit through a binary-safe script (the pre-commit check caught both).
- The scratchpad directory is shared with other agents' sessions: a regress output named regress1.txt was overwritten
  by another run. Name outputs after the agent.
