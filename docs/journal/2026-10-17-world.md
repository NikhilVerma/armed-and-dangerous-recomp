## Learned

- The worktree tools: heredocs and backticks inside `python -c` are refused by the sandbox; edit through a patch file
  (old / new blocks, line endings kept). `sed -i` on a CRLF file strips the CRs here: avoid on CRLF files.
- Jolt contacts report the solver's velocities after the step; the original's collision callback reads them before:
  keep last frame's velocities (movable.rs `before`) or every crush becomes a push.
- The goliath is 20 units tall in its model space and in the world (no placement scale): the old capsule (64 tall) was
  three times its size.
- Instance props matter: 30 of lev_1_m1's trees are "valid" (the property sets say 0), and the barracks' ruin keeps
  the barracks' reference anim and so its exit slot.
