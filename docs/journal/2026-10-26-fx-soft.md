# 2026-10-26: soft fx cards and the missing impact smoke (branch fix/fx-soft)

Two effects artefacts in lev_bonus_m1, where the turrets now fire at Roman (work/turret/zoom.jpg,
work/review/turret_bm1.jpg).

## 1. The fireball's hard horizontal edge

The fireball is `fxo_spark_anim`, the second object of `fx_imp_stig` (the turret bullets' impact): a 1 x 1 camera
card (0x2e) scaled 3.5..4.2 (0x0d), an 8-frame texture animation (0x63), drawn additively (FxAddMaterial,
fxadd.wesl). It stands on the ground, so the depth test cuts it along the line where it meets the terrain.

The original does the same. Its walk frames show the cut on every impact fireball (lev_bonus_m1 original frame 165:
two fireballs cut flat at the bottom; work/fxsoft/o165_c.png). The original draws the cards with no depth bias and
no soft edge. The soft particles (graphics.rs `soft_particles`, particle.wesl) only covered the particle pools.

Fix (remaster, behind `soft_particles`, so the Original preset keeps the hard line): fxadd.wesl fades a card's alpha
by its depth gap to the prepass depth, as particle.wesl does. The fade depth is 0.3 of the card's own size (world
units per unit of its mesh uvs, from the screen derivatives), at most the particles' depth (graphics.rs
`CARD_SOFTNESS`). A fixed depth did not work for both sizes. At the particles' 6 units the 4-unit fireball dimmed
whole (first try). At 1.2 units the 20-unit flash `fxo_flash_2` (same fx, scale 20) kept a hard line. A card behind
a terrain crest is still cut by the crest's silhouette (walk frame 210, work/fxsoft/flash_zoom.png): that is real
occlusion, not the card meeting the ground.

## 2. The "cauliflower" smoke

There were two causes, and only one of them was a bug.

(a) The impact smoke was never drawn. `fx_imp_stig` / `fx_imp_flemming` start their smoke emitter (pool 1, the grey
puff of n_fx_bank_2 at u 0.51..0.74, v 0.626..0.874, size 1..2 growing 1..1.5/s, life 2..3 s) on an `fxo_ref_sphere`
that lives 0.01 s. The remaster aged a new object by at least 1/60 s on its first update. So a 0.01 s object ended
before its emitters ran, and the impacts made no smoke at all. Measured: the pool-1 count did not move after an
fx_imp_stig.

The original does not age the object on that update. Fx_CreateObject 0x004234d0 sets obj+0x468 = fx+0xc - dt (the
script's carried time, 0 for a new fx, minus the frame time; game/fx.cpp:171). fn_0041a350 0x0041a350 ages the object
by dt + +0x468 = 0 (render_r1.cpp:1061). The emitters still step with the frame's dt (0x0041a050 reads
[0x00604a90]), so the object emits one puff and ends on its second update. docs/subsystems/fx-vm.md "First update
time" already said so.

Fix: object.rs `FxObject::updated`; the first update ages the object by 0. This affects every object with a
lifespan under a frame: about 55 scripts in lev_1_m1's bank. They are the bullet impacts (stig, flemming, sticky,
"impact_small"), the snow footsteps, and several explosions (keg, oil pump, tree, mortar, base building). Each now
emits what its script asks for on that one update. Those emitters had never run in the remaster.

(b) The dark "cauliflower" clumps and the dark smudge are not smoke. They come from `fx_exp_generic`, the
end-of-flight fx of the rocket captains' rockets (p_captain_rocket) and the forge cannon bullets. Its first emitter
throws about 200 dirt clods: n_fx_bank_2 at u 0.01..0.124, v 0.51..0.624, a dark rusty tile with an opaque oval
alpha, size 0.1..0.4, speed 20..30, interval 0.001 for 0.2 s. Its second emitter throws a black speckle dust (the top
right tile, size 5..10, life 5 s). Shown on their own in work/fxsoft/pt/gen_sheet.jpg. The Original preset draws them
the same way, and the data and the draw match the original's code (pool 1 = manager+0x20, n_fx_bank_2, alpha blend;
Fx_LoadChunk 0x0041c7e0, Particles_Draw 0x0046d9f0). In the walk clip, about 30 of these blasts land around Roman
in 10 s. The original clip shows none: its Roman dies at frame 150 and no rocket lands in view. Whether that many
rockets should come is a question for the rocket captains' timing and aim (fix/turret-range), not for the renderer.
No original frame of an fx_exp_generic exists to compare the clods against.
