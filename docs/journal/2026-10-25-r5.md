## Fixed

1. **Objects between the camera and Roman stayed opaque: characters were never hit by the camera's rays.**
   Camera_Update 0x0040e56c fades everything its five rays pass through, characters included (their collision
   models). The engine's rays met physics bodies only. A grunt's body is a sphere at its feet, which a ray from
   Roman's head passes over, and the companions are Jolt walkers outside the physics world. So Q on lev_5_m3 (walk
   frames 285-299) filled the view with his magnified armour plates and cape. Those were the "flat grey / black /
   dark red polygons": the textures were fine, only magnified and cut by the near plane. Now the rays also test the
   grunts' hit volumes (Physics::hit_volumes_along) and the companions' collision parts (hitparts::hit,
   hitparts::near_segment shared with the bullets). Q's cape is its own entity and takes his alpha
   (camfade::AlphaFollows; Character_SetFade 0x0042eb60 fades a character's attachments). Sheet:
   work/r5/lev_5_m3/fade_before_after.jpg.
   The lev_2_m2 pipe (frames 240-299) is **not a fade case**: it is beside the camera, not between it and Roman. The
   remaster's Roman walks past the barrels where the original's stops at them, so the camera ends up next to the
   pipe. Its smooth, washed look was the specular (item 5); with power 8 per vertex it keeps its rust (sheet
   work/r5/lev_2_m2/pipe.jpg).
2. **lev_4_m2 frame 0, Roman's head, sleeves, gloves and boots flat white: an asynchronous pipeline compile.** The
   pixels are exactly (180, 197, 213), lev_4_m2's fog colour, which is the clear colour. With the Remaster's depth
   prepass, a mesh whose prepass pipeline was ready but whose game material colour pipeline was still compiling wrote
   depth and no colour for that frame. It is timing-dependent: 1 level of 19 in the integration capture, and it did
   not happen in a rerun. Fix: `RenderPlugin::synchronous_pipeline_compilation` (main.rs). A pipeline is created in
   the frame that first needs it. Capture times are unchanged (24-26 s per level walk). The other levels' frame 0 was
   checked in the integration frames (Roman box): no fog-coloured patches.
3. **HUD player figure too transparent: the HUD blended in linear light.** b_health on ui_health is opacity 0.65,
   SRCALPHA / INVSRCALPHA (0x004897d0, blend table 0x0057d9e0 entry 3). D3D9 blends the stored gamma bytes. The figure
   interior over lev_1_m1_b's snow measured (123, 186, 111) before, (118, 185, 66) after, against the original's
   (114, 182, 65). The HUD camera composites with `CompositingSpace::Srgb`. The game view always draws into its
   picture while a HUD camera exists (display/render_scale.rs), because Bevy honours the space only for a stack of 2D
   cameras. Side effect, fixed: bevy_egui's automatic context landed on the HUD camera and wgpu quit on the format
   mismatch (hud playtest at 14.4 s). The egui context now goes on the game view's camera, and automatic creation is
   off. Sheet: work/r5/lev_1_m1_b/hud_before_after.jpg.
5. **Jump pack (lev_3_m1 / lev_3_m2) dark instead of chrome: the specular power.** RenderModes_Load 0x00489b40
   (loader_01.cpp:1910) replaces every record's power with 8.0 when the record has a power and a specular colour. The
   characters' bump + specular pixel shader ps_731b0a29671b0f18 uses (N.H)^8 too (three squarings). The engine used
   the records' powers (25 on the dome), so the highlight was a dot. The fixed-function specular is now also per
   vertex, as the original's lighting and the skinned vertex shaders (vs_0002cde5b3229591, `lit`) compute it. The pack
   is **not** an environment material (kind 1, no texture2), so commit a7345ec (no env map marker -> the cube stage
   adds black) is not involved. Sheet: work/r5/lev_3_m1/pack_spec.jpg (the dome and nozzles now silver).

## Open

- Jump pack's thin orange / tan strips at the wings (submesh 10 region; the original's are dark teal). Lead: the pack's
  mesh names its texture 'N_T_iron' while the level's bank holds 'N_t_iron'. The bank's hash is case-insensitive
  (HashTable_HashString 0x004f1100), but its compare callback 0x00488150 (RenderSystem_LoadTextures) is not
  recovered. If that compare is case-sensitive, the original draws the membranes untextured: white x the teal diffuse
  (0.16, 0.33, 0.31), which is what its frames show. To settle it: recover 0x00488150, or take a material log
  (`[test] log_materials`) on lev_3_m1 (needs the human's OK for an original run). The fix would then be in the
  exporter (case-sensitive texture match), followed by a re-export.

## Learned

- A flat silhouette in exactly the level's fog colour means a prepass with no colour pass: a pipeline not ready yet,
  or the far clip (graphics/farclip.rs).
- Bevy 0.20 has `CompositingSpace` for gamma-space 2D blending. It does nothing in a stack that holds a 3D camera.
- bevy_egui keys its pipeline on hdr only (Rgba8UnormSrgb otherwise). Keep it off cameras with a different main format.
- RenderModes_Load rewrites material records at load (translucency bit 0x10, the specular power 8). Check it before
  trusting a record's stored value.
