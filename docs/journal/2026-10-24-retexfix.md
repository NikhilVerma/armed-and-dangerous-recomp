  tools/remaster_textures.py locked every override texel-exactly to the original (cand + up(orig - down(cand))): a 2x

- **The villagers' panel and the HUD.** hud.rs loaded ui/textures/*.png (the UI export) and never the retextures.
  graphics::ui_texture_path gives a HUD texture its retexture when the bank stores it with straight alpha (n_peasant,

## Learned / open

- **TAA gets no motion vectors and no depth on levels without the GPU snowfall** (work/retexfix/dbg_*: a debug
  patch of Bevy's taa.wesl output length(motion) in red, 1/history_confidence in green, closest depth > 0 in blue).
  lev_1_m1 with snow: motion and depth present; lev_1_m1 with `snow=false`, lev_3_m1, lev_3_m3, lev_5_m2: all zero,
  history confidence maxed, so TAA blends 98.5 % un-reprojected history and smears the moving image. This is most of
  lev_5_m2's "blurrier" ground: ground |Laplacian| at play frame 190 TAA 5.9, SMAA 11.3, MSAA 14.3 (still frame: TAA
  = MSAA). Ruled out: Core3d set ordering (strong chain), view respecialisation (NormalPrepass / a dummy component
  added later), NoIndirectDrawing, the env atlas, the sky, the remaster settings one by one (AO, soft particles, far
  fade, bloom, env maps, tessellation, motion blur, lod, dither, fog, ACES), StandardMaterial landscape. The prepass
  opaque phase is not empty. Bisected on lev_5_m2 with the snowfall forced on (a debug switch in snow.rs `track`):
  TAA's inputs become valid; with the snow pass's draw skipped, or only its compute pass, or only its render pass,
  they are zero again; an empty render pass after TAA, or a no-op system ordered after it, changes nothing. So
  something about the full snow pass (compute + draw binding the prepass depth, after TAA) makes TAA read the
  frame's prepass; without it TAA reads zeros. Not resolved; the same could starve SSAO / motion blur / soft
  particles on those levels. The debug switches were removed (not committed).
- Ground sharpness, lev_5_m2 (mean |Laplacian| of the ground region at 960x558; the original from its walk video,
  H.264): frame 70 original 5.92, before 5.27, after 6.14; frame 190 8.67 / 6.34 / 7.30; frame 310 6.09 / 5.24 /
  6.41. Per unit of brightness (the remaster is darker) the remaster now exceeds the original in all three, but the
  TAA smear (radial streaks while walking) stays until the fault above is fixed.
- Pack textures regenerated with Media Lab (the recipe's SeedVR2 7b faithful route + recipe.finish, for the six
  that failed the gate): only fx_glass_shattered passes now (docs/retexture.md). Every texture without a retexture
  is accounted for in work/retexfix/coverage.md; the others are by design (under 32 px, data, normal maps, font).
