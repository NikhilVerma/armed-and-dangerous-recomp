# 2026-10-26 (agent-keg-end): where the lev_2_m2 walk ends at the kegs

Follow-up to docs/journal/2026-10-26-steep-slope.md. The walk video (tools/compare_videos.py, W from play frame 10
for 620 frames; video frame i = play frame 10 + 2i) still differed in frames 240-299: the original's Roman ends right
of the two red kegs (powderkegs 156 / 157 before snow_pipe 192) with the camera at normal distance; the remaster's
was wedged between the kegs at (-9.1, -181.6) with the camera pushed in.

## Root cause 1 (fixed): Roman's heading drifts in the original, not in the remaster

The original's trace of the lev_2_m2 start walk (work/oracle/lev_2_m2_start/original.jsonl, W 120..240) shows the
heading turning 168.50 -> 169.12 degrees with no turn input; the remaster held 168.50. Over the video's ~460 walk
frames that is ~2-4 units sideways at the kegs: the remaster's path met keg 157 1.5 units on its +x side and glanced
into the gap, the original's passes it on the -x side (screen right of both kegs).

Mechanism (PlayerObject_Update 0x004788a0 at 0x00479e2e): every frame the original reads Roman's facing back from his
Havok body (Object_GetNegColumn1 0x00453150), adds the turn input, writes a yaw-only placement (Object_SetTransform
0x00457990) and zeroes the angular velocity (Body_setAngularVelocity 0x00458580). Whatever the physics step turned
the body about the vertical stays. The body is a solid sphere (Object_BuildRadiusCollision 0x004527d0: sphere mass
properties, inertia 2/5 m r^2); the ground friction impulse acts at the contact, r below the centre along -n, and on
a side slope its moment has a vertical part: (n x v)_z.

Evidence: all level-start walks of the original (work/oracle/*_start) against the landscape's normal under Roman
(scratch fit, landscape.bin triangles): the heading change per frame follows -(n x v_hat)_z with one sign everywhere;
lev_2_m2 +0.62, lev_4_m1 +0.27, lev_2_m1 +0.13, lev_3_m1 +0.08, lev_2_m3 -0.10, lev_5_m1 -0.11, lev_5_m4 -0.07, flat
starts ~0. Steeper side slopes turn him less per unit of tilt (less friction impulse: normal z smaller, none at <= 0.7).

Change (engine/src/player.rs): `ground_contact` returns its friction velocity change; `friction_turn` turns r.yaw by
FRICTION_TURN_SHARE x (5 / (2 r^2)) (r_c x dv).y x dt. The share 0.0165 is fitted (Havok's solve is not ported; the
full rigid-sphere moment would be ~60x too much). Remaster vs original heading change, play frames 120 -> 250 of the
start walks (work/slope/hd.py):

| level | original | remaster |
| --- | --- | --- |
| lev_2_m2 | +0.63 | +0.62 |
| lev_4_m1 | +0.28 | +0.26 |
| lev_5_m1 | -0.11 | -0.12 |
| lev_2_m3 | -0.10 | -0.10 |
| lev_2_m1 | +0.13 | +0.14 |
| lev_3_m1 | +0.08 | +0.09 |
| lev_5_m4 | -0.07 | -0.04 |
| lev_bonus_m3 | +0.42 | 0.00 (share 0.02 run) |
| lev_1_m3 | -0.09 | 0.00 (share 0.02 run) |

lev_bonus_m3 and lev_1_m3 turn in the original although the
landscape under the walk is flat: he walks on objects there (bridge / mesh), whose contacts this does not see yet:
the turn uses only the floor contact the update keeps (+0x574), not each contact's friction.

Result on the video (work/review/keg_end/orig_before_after.jpg: columns original | before | after, rows video frames
200 / 220 / 240 / 270 / 299; full-resolution crops original_/remaster_0220/0240/0299_crop.png): frames 200-240 now
match the original's framing (Roman to the right of both kegs, camera at normal distance, kegs at the same screen
places within a few pixels).

## Root cause 2 (open): the original's Roman is held behind grunt 29, the remaster's goes round him

From play frame ~500 (video 245) the two part again. The original's Roman stays where he is to the end (frames
240-299 nearly static), with the archer grunt 29 (at (-10.3, -196.0, -64.7), radius 4, standing still in both) right in
front of him. The remaster's meets grunt 29's sphere at play frame 502 and slides along the crease between that sphere
and the side slope (contact normals logged with a temporary walker dump: the grunt's normal horizontal, then tilting
up; the floor normal z 0.77 rising ahead, then 0.51), rises ~5 units over the sphere's side by 516 and walks on to the
foot of snow_pipe 192 (7, -263): the camera then sits against the pipe (frames 260-299). The camera is not at fault:
it follows Roman's position (work/review/keg_end/seq_after1.jpg).

Why the original's Havok body does not take that crease is not settled: candidates are Roman pushing the grunt's
dynamic body (Jolt's CharacterVirtual treats it as immovable here), the original's list shape (three spheres,
PlayerObject_BuildCollision 0x00475220) against the remaster's capsule, or the drive's s^5 step along the crease.
Settling it needs the original's positions there: tools/oracle_scenarios/lev_2_m2_kegs.json (the video's keys,
traced from play frame 400; needs the human's OK to run the original) gives Roman and grunt 29 every 2 frames.
