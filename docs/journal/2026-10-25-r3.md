## Learned

- The fx-object material state word: 0x10 fog, 0x20 z-write off, 0x40 z-func always, 0x100.. blend (0x00505320
  Renderer_ApplyMaterial). Fx_SetBackground sets 0x20 (+ fog per its word); BackgroundObject_Load only the fog bit.
- Two @fragment entry points in one WESL module need an explicit entry point in `specialize`; otherwise the pipeline
  fails (logged as a validation error) and the material draws nothing. The first frame or two of a capture can be the
  clear colour while new pipelines compile (lev_5_m1 frame 0 was once flat grey; a rerun was fine).
- The original video frames' chat line at y 437 sits in the letterbox bar, so the captures crop the first chat line.
