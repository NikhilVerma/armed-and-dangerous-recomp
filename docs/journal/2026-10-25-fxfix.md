  the artefacts are real though:
  - the big white haze with the stair-stepped green / yellow rim: fx_imp_stig's fxo_flash_2 (a camera card, 0x2e,
    scale 20, opacity 0.5, 0.1 s), visible since fe57514; gone with FXSKIP=fx_imp_stig (A3_diag_*f0260*). Its faint

    (~0.15) it adds about 2.5-4x the original's light. Fix proposal: add the additive fx in gamma space over the real
    destination (an fx-add accumulation buffer composited over the scene), not a fixed match brightness.
  - the orange discs with dotted rings on every tracer: fx_trail_stig's fxo_glow_red camera cards (scale 10); the
    pack's fx_glow_red retexture is broken (a black ring, holes, speckled rim: work/fxfix/cards_x6.png, original vs
    pack, x6). Pack issue (tools/remaster_textures.py, section B).
  - the blast ring (fxo_blast_ring_snow) and the particle bank are not the cause (FXSKIP / exclude tests).
