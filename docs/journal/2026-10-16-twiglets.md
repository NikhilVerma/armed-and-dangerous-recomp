  on the AI list with their flags at +0x460, which would have read as a grunt state).
- twiglet.rs (TwigletPlugin): Generator (from the tree's props and its reference anim's mover nodes, level.rs), Swarm
  (TwigletController_Slot47: a twiglet every 0.1..0.25 s from the active tree with the most left, timer rolled at the
  level start), the twiglet's states 1 / 0x32 / 0x0a / 0x46 / 0x1e / 0x1f, the get_bomb note, the bomb set off within
  10 units, the leash, deaths (bomb let go and set off; ordered after fx.rs and before level.rs's husks, which despawn
  the object: before that order the death was missed). fx.rs `BlastProof`: a twiglet carrying a bomb ignores area
  damage (Twiglet_TakeDamage: flag 0x40 with +0x55c), so a blast lights the carried bombs and the crowd goes off in a
  0.5 s chain, as the original's (bombs at 1300, 1328, 1358, 1386, 1416, 1444).
- The run's speed: the original's lone runners make 36-38 units / s on the flat and crawl uphill (27 at 6 degrees, 19 at
  9, ~10 at 12-15). The drive (0x00454d80) is a mass x 50 velocity force whose wanted vertical is 0 while rising, on a
  wood body (wood_friction 20): `ground_speed` is its steady state with a fitted friction 2.0. Tried first: Jolt
  friction on the body (20, 100: twiglets jam each other and stop on the flat; Jolt is not Havok), a velocity
  controller with Jolt friction 0.8 (no slope effect). With the model the leader's distance to Roman agrees within 10
  units each second to frame 1200.
- Monk summon (states 0xf2 / 0xf3): ai/monk.rs `Spell`, `summon_point`, `twiglet_spots`; ai.rs casts it (anim 0x25,
  turned to the point) and writes SpawnTwiglet at the cast's end; charanim plays the cast's own anim. Playtest step
  `monk_spell`.
- oracle_diff: families twiglets.alive / .emerging / .running / .bombs (count crossing within twiglet_frames 30) and
