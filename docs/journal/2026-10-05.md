## Shadow pop-in fixed at the source

- Recovered the scene pass (src/renderer/scene_pass.cpp): `ScenePass_Render` 0x00489070,
  `Frustum_FromViewProjection` 0x004889a0, `World_GatherFrustum` 0x0048eab0, `ScenePass_DrawLayers` 0x00488e90,
  `ScenePass_ResetStages` 0x0048a1a0. Enhancement point `g_sceneCullRadius` (`[render] cull_radius`, remaster 100):
  the main pass's side and near planes move out by R, so nothing within R of the camera is culled; the renderer's
  caster cache is then off (rhi::SceneHints::shadowCasterCache). Not yet looked at in game.
