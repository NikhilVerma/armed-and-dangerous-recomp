Generated by `python tools/port_queue.py --deferred --md <this file>` from docs/port/*.csv (rows with disposition `deferred`), docs/port/features.csv, work/coverage.json (the levels whose coverage runs execute a function, not counting functions every level reaches: those are load / per-frame checks) and work/remaster/<level>/scene.json (the levels that place a feature's templates). Ranked by effect, then by the number of levels whose objective involves the feature, then by the number of levels it affects. Effect: **blocks objective** (a level cannot be finished as in the original), **visible gameplay** (missing behaviour the player sees), **unknown** (not traced), **cosmetic** (looks / sound only). Objective levels: the levels whose mission objective involves the feature's objects (mission.json start texts, trigger chains). Dispositions: docs/port/README.md.
